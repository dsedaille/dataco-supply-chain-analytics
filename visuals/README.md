# Visuals

This folder contains exported charts and visualizations used in the README, reports, and portfolio presentation.

## Included Visuals

- `late_delivery_rate_by_shipping_mode.png`  
  Shows late delivery rate by shipping mode.

- `late_delivery_rate_by_customer_segment.png`  
  Compares late delivery rates across customer segments.

- `late_delivery_rate_by_market.png`  
  Compares late delivery rates across geographic markets.

- `top_10_order_regions_by_late_delivery_rate.png`  
  Shows the order regions with the highest late delivery rates.

- `top_10_product_categories_by_delivery_rate.png`  
  Shows the product categories with the highest late delivery rates.

- `top_10_product_categories_by_total_sales.png`  
  Shows the top product categories by total sales.

- `top_10_product_categories_by_total_profit.png`  
  Shows the top product categories by total profit.

- `top_10_product_categories_by_avg_profit_margin.png`  
  Shows the top product categories by average profit margin.

- `high_margin_categories_order_volume_vs_margin.png`  
  Compares order volume and profit margin for high-margin categories.

- `discount_rate_vs_profit_margin_by_category.png`  
  Shows the relationship between average discount rate and average profit margin.

- `order_count_by_order_status.png`  
  Shows the number of orders by order status.

- `late_delivery_rate_by_order_status.png`  
  Shows late delivery rate by order status.

- `total_sales_by_order_status.png`  
  Shows total sales by order status.

- `total_profit_by_order_status.png`  
  Shows total profit by order status.

- `operational_priority_by_order_status.png`  
  Shows order share and late delivery risk by active or fulfilled order status.
